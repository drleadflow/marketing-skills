# Worked example: YouTube video plan (fictional home-services company)

Business: Lindqvist Heating & Air (fictional). Core promise: "Here's exactly what a fair HVAC quote looks like, so you stop guessing."

## Research (step 1)

Search results for "how to read an HVAC quote" lean heavily on the benefit angle ("how to"). No past videos exist yet for this channel, so there's no retention graph to pull; default checkpoints apply until a few videos are live.

## Titles

1. Curiosity: "What Your HVAC Quote Isn't Telling You"
2. Benefit: "How to Read an HVAC Quote Like a Pro (In 10 Minutes)"
3. Specific-number: "3 Numbers Every HVAC Quote Should Have"

## Thumbnail concept

Text: "3 NUMBERS" in bold, high-contrast over a close-up of a real quote sheet with a pen. No face needed; the document itself is the curiosity object.

## Thumbnail test plan

Variant A: "3 NUMBERS" text. Variant B: same image, no text, face reacting to the quote instead. Testing whether the text or the expression is doing the work.

## First 15 seconds (written, not summarized)

"If your HVAC quote doesn't have these 3 numbers on it, you're flying blind. I'm going to show you exactly what to look for, and it's faster than you'd think."
