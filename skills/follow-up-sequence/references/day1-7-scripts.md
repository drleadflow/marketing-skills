# Day 1 to 7 scripts

Fill-in drafts for the front-loaded week in SKILL.md step 1. Day 1 is covered in detail in **speed-to-lead**'s `references/first-hour-scripts.md`; this file picks up day 2 onward, plus the two concern-routed variants referenced from day 1. Fictional businesses: Clearhead Coaching (business coach), Ridgeline HVAC (home services), Willow Aesthetics (med spa).

## Clearhead Coaching

**Day 2 AM, SMS (social proof, only send if it's true that day; otherwise skip to the day 2 PM touch)**
> {first_name}, just wrapped a call with someone working through almost the same pricing question. Happy to share what we found if useful, no pitch, just the breakdown.

If no real call like that happened, don't send this touch; use a true, general version instead, or a proof-ledger item with `Proof used: P-004`-style sourcing: "A lot of the solo coaches we work with hit this same pricing question. Happy to share what usually fixes it, no pitch, just the breakdown." Never invent a specific event that didn't happen.

**Day 2 PM, email (objection-answer content)**
> Subject: "I don't have time for an audit right now"
>
> {first_name}, the audit itself is 20 minutes, not a project. Most people walk out with one change they can make that same day. Here's what that looked like for {proof_example}: {link}.

**Day 3 AM, call attempt**
> "Hey {first_name}, {rep_name} again, wanted to share something relevant to the pricing question you mentioned. Call me back whenever, or I'll follow up by text."

**Day 3 PM, SMS (direct ask)**
> Would {day} at {time} work for a quick 20 minutes, {first_name}? {booking_link}

**Day 4, email (longer value piece)**
> Subject: the 3-part pricing fix most solo coaches skip
>
> Full breakdown here: {link}. No ask attached, just useful either way.

**Day 5, SMS**
> Still thinking about the pricing stuff, {first_name}? Happy to help whenever the timing's right, no rush.

**Day 6, email (case study)**
> Subject: how {proof_example} fixed this in a week
> {short_case_study_summary}. {booking_link} if you want the same breakdown.

**Day 7, final SMS**
> Last check-in from me, {first_name}. If you ever want to revisit this, just reply here anytime.

## Concern-routed variants (any day, any business)

**"Not the right time"**
> No worries at all, {first_name}. Want me to check back in a couple weeks, or would you rather reach out when it's better timing on your end?

Log the answer. If they name a date, pause every scheduled message until that date and assign a human to follow up then.

**"Too expensive"**
> Appreciate you telling me straight, {first_name}. Can I ask what would feel like a fair range? There might be a version that fits, might not, but I'd rather give you a real answer than keep pitching the same thing.

Never discount in the reply itself; this message is for finding out whether the gap is price, timing, or scope before deciding anything.
