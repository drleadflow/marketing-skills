# Carousel writer

You'll finish with a complete carousel: a cover slide, 5 to 7 interior slides, and a CTA slide, each slide's copy written and checked against the 4 copy tests before anyone touches layout.

## Before you start

Read `.agents/business-context.md` and `.agents/proof-ledger.md` if they exist. If business-context is missing, offer to run **business-context** first, or ask only what's missing:

1. What's the one idea this carousel teaches? (One idea, not three. If there are three, that's three carousels.)
2. Is there a real lead magnet or next step to point to on the last slide?
3. Does the business have proof (a stat, a before/after, a quote) in the ledger that fits this topic?

## How to do it

### 1. Pick the carousel type

| Type | Shape |
|---|---|
| Myth vs. truth | Cover states the myth, interior slides correct it piece by piece |
| Framework | Cover promises a named system, interior slides are steps 1 through N |
| Before/after | Cover shows the gap, interior slides walk the transformation |
| List | Cover promises N things, each interior slide is one item, no filler slides |
| Story | Cover opens the loop, interior slides build the narrative to a turn |

### 2. Write the cover slide (this IS the hook)

Use **hook-writer** to generate and score the opening line. The cover carries:
- A kicker (small label, category or tag, optional)
- The headline (the hook itself, one strong accent word if the design supports it)
- A subline (optional, one supporting clause, never repeats the headline)

Unlike video, ignore any "safe zone for a face" rule here; treat the whole slide as the hook.

### 3. Write 5 to 7 interior slides, one idea per slide

**Every slide pays rent.** Each one must do ONE of: give a definition, give an analogy, state a rule, give a number, give a template, or give a test the reader can run on themselves. A slide that just restates the headline or adds a transition sentence with no new teaching gets cut.

Vary slide types across the deck so it doesn't read as one long list:
- **Statement slide**: one bold claim, minimal text
- **Quote/field-note slide**: a short, specific insight framed as a note from experience
- **Before/after chip slide**: crossed-out old belief vs. the new one
- **List slide**: 2 to 4 short bullet points, not paragraphs
- **Steps slide**: numbered 01/02/03, each a short phrase

### 4. Write the CTA slide

- Restate identity (who this is from, in one line) if the design uses a name/brand chip.
- One body line maximum, no re-explaining the whole carousel.
- One CTA: "Comment [KEYWORD] for [named, real resource]" or "Save this for [specific future moment]" or "Follow for [specific content pillar]."
- Match the CTA to the goal: comment-trigger for a resource handoff, save for a reference piece, follow for pure authority-building.
- Never a swipe-arrow reminder on the last slide; there's nowhere left to swipe to.

### 5. Run the 4 copy tests before layout

Every slide must pass all 4 before it goes to design:

| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| Scroll-stop | Would slide 1 alone stop someone mid-scroll? |
| Retention | Does each slide create enough curiosity about the next one to keep swiping? |
| Save | Is there at least one slide worth screenshotting or saving for later? |
| Comment | Does the CTA slide give a real, specific reason to comment (not "thoughts?")? |

### 6. Hand to layout with a word audit

Before anything renders: cut repeated words across slides (a kicker that restates the headline, a tag repeated twice), fix orphan words, and confirm no slide has more text than a reader can take in during a 2 to 3 second swipe. Interior slides that are busy with text are a design failure, not a copy win.

## Check it

- [ ] One idea per carousel, stated in a single sentence before writing
- [ ] Cover slide passes the hook-writer Pattern-Interrupt gate (2+ of 4)
- [ ] Every interior slide does one job (definition/analogy/rule/number/template/test); none are filler
- [ ] All 4 copy tests pass (scroll-stop, retention, save, comment)
- [ ] CTA points to a real resource that exists, not a placeholder
- [ ] Zero em dashes or en dashes anywhere, no banned/hype words
- [ ] Every stat or claim traces to the proof ledger or is marked `[NEEDS PROOF: ...]`
- [ ] Under the finished carousel, list `Proof used: P-xxx, P-xxx` naming every approved claim ID relied on, or "Proof used: none"

## Rules

- Never reuse the exact cover hook or cover photo/visual concept from the account's previous carousel.
- Never show a before/after claim without the permission and sourcing a real before/after requires; use `[NEEDS PROOF: ...]` until cleared.
- Health, money, legal, and before/after claims follow **health-claims-check** when the business is health-related.
- No real client names, patient stories, or screenshots in anything meant for this repo's examples; use clearly fictional businesses.
