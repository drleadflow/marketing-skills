# Worked example: myth-buster carousel (fictional med spa)

Business: Wren Aesthetics (fictional). One idea: the "financing means it costs more" myth, corrected. This topic is deliberately non-clinical; any slide that touches a treatment claim instead of a pricing/policy claim routes through health-claims-check before publishing for real.

## Slide 1: Cover
Kicker: BEHIND THE PRICE
Headline: "Financing doesn't mean you're paying more."
Subline: "Here's what's happening."

## Slide 2: Statement
"The myth: if you finance instead of paying up front, you end up paying extra just for the privilege of splitting it up."

## Slide 3: Rule
"The rule: our financing partner charges 0% interest on approved credit. The total you pay financed is the same total you'd pay up front, just spread out."

## Slide 4: Analogy
"Think of it like a layaway plan, not a loan. You're not borrowing against the future, you're just choosing when the money leaves your account."

## Slide 5: Number
"[NEEDS PROOF: cite the real approval rate and average plan length from the clinic's own financing partner before publishing this slide for a real client]"

## Slide 6: Template
"The fix: ask for the total cost financed and the total cost up front, side by side, before you decide. If they're not the same number, ask why."

## Slide 7: CTA
Name chip: Wren Aesthetics
Body: "We get this question in almost every consult."
