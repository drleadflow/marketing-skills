# Sample quiz, scoring, and results variants

A full worked example for a fictional business coach, Clearhead Coaching, called the "Growth Bottleneck Finder." 7 questions across 4 dimensions: offer clarity, lead volume, follow-up speed, close rate. Swap dimensions and questions for a different business; keep the scoring mechanic.

## Questions

**Q1 (offer clarity).** When someone asks what you charge, how quickly can you answer?
- A. Instantly, one number (5)
- B. I have a range, it depends (3)
- C. I usually have to think about it (1)

**Q2 (offer clarity).** Do people usually understand what they're getting before they buy?
- A. Yes, it's written down clearly (5)
- B. Mostly, but I explain it live (3)
- C. Honestly, not really (1)

**Q3 (lead volume).** How many new leads did you get last month?
- A. More than I can follow up with (5)
- B. A steady, manageable number (3)
- C. Fewer than 5 (1)

**Q4 (lead volume).** Where do most of your leads come from?
- A. A consistent, repeatable channel (5)
- B. A mix, nothing consistent (3)
- C. Mostly referrals I can't control (1)

**Q5 (follow-up speed).** How fast do you usually reply to a new lead?
- A. Within minutes (5)
- B. Same day (3)
- C. Whenever I get to it (1)

**Q6 (follow-up speed).** How many follow-up touches do you send before giving up on a lead?
- A. A planned sequence, several touches (5)
- B. One or two, then I forget (3)
- C. Just the first reply (1)

**Q7 (close rate).** When someone says "let me think about it," what usually happens?
- A. I follow up and most convert eventually (5)
- B. Some convert, most go quiet (3)
- C. They almost never come back (1)

## Scoring

Score every dimension as an average of its mapped questions, on the same 1-to-5 scale, so a 1-question dimension and a 2-question dimension are comparable:

| Dimension | Questions | How it's scored | Average range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offer clarity | Q1, Q2 | (Q1 + Q2) ÷ 2 | 1 to 5 |
| Lead volume | Q3, Q4 | (Q3 + Q4) ÷ 2 | 1 to 5 |
| Follow-up speed | Q5, Q6 | (Q5 + Q6) ÷ 2 | 1 to 5 |
| Close rate | Q7 | Q7 itself | 1 to 5 |

Worked example: a respondent answers B, A, B, C, A, B, B. That's offer clarity (3+5)/2=4, lead volume (3+1)/2=2, follow-up speed (5+3)/2=4, close rate 3. Lowest average is lead volume at 2, so that's the headline result, not close rate, even though close rate's raw single-question score (3) looks lower than a two-question sum would. Ties break toward whichever dimension the business considers the current chokepoint (see **chokepoint-audit**).
