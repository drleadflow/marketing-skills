```markdown
# SOP: Process a same-day appointment cancellation (fictional)

- Purpose: Recover the slot and follow the clinic's deposit policy consistently.
- Owner: Front desk lead | Last updated: 2026-10-11 | Estimated time: 10 min | Frequency: per event

## Prerequisites
- Tools: scheduling system, payment processor
- Permissions: front desk access to refunds
- Info to gather: patient name, appointment time, reason given

## Step-by-step procedure
1. **Step 1: Confirm the cancellation request**
   - Instruction: Ask the patient to confirm in writing (text or email) which appointment they're cancelling.
   - Expected outcome: Written confirmation with date/time.
   - Time estimate: 2 min
2. **Step 2: Check the deposit policy window**
   - Instruction: Open the scheduling system, find the appointment, check hours until the visit.
   - Expected outcome: You know if this falls inside or outside the 24-hour deposit window.
   - Time estimate: 2 min
3. **Step 3: Apply the correct outcome**
   - Instruction: Outside 24 hours, refund deposit in full via payment processor. Inside 24 hours, apply deposit as credit toward rebooking per written policy.
   - Expected outcome: Refund or credit is processed.
   - Time estimate: 3 min

## Decision points
- IF the cancellation is more than 24 hours before the appointment, THEN refund in full (step 3a). OTHERWISE apply as rebooking credit (step 3b).

## Quality checks
- CHECK: refund or credit amount matches the deposit on file. Expected: exact match. If wrong: escalate to office manager before processing.

## Troubleshooting
| Error | Fix | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Payment processor won't refund | Note reason, apply as account credit instead | Office manager |

## Completion criteria
- [ ] Slot released in scheduling system
- [ ] Refund or credit processed and logged
- Logged in: patient note in scheduling system
```
