# Example magnet outline

A full worked outline for a fictional business coach, Clearhead Coaching: "Price Your First Offer," a one-page template (decision-stage format, per SKILL.md step 1). This is the same resource referenced in **comment-to-dm**'s `references/keyword-example.md`.

## Format

One-page worksheet, Google Doc or PDF, usable within 5 minutes of opening.

## Headline

"Price Your First Offer: A 10-Minute Worksheet"

## What's inside (3 to 5 bullets shown on the delivery page)

- The one question to ask before you pick a number
- A simple math check that tells you if your price covers your time
- 3 pricing mistakes that make people say "let me think about it"
- A fill-in template to land on your actual number today

## The worksheet content itself

1. **Section 1, "What are you solving?"** One line, in the client's own words, not a feature list.
2. **Section 2, "The math check."** A simple formula: hours the deliverable takes, times a target hourly rate, plus a margin for the thinking time that happens outside sessions. Shows a worked example with placeholder numbers, not a real client's numbers.
3. **Section 3, "Pick your number."** A fill-in blank with 3 anchor examples (low, middle, high) so the reader isn't staring at a blank field.
4. **Section 4, "Say it out loud."** A one-line script for stating the price without hedging, to rehearse before the next sales call.

## Delivery page (what the download link opens to)

- Confirms what they're getting in one sentence
- The worksheet itself, embedded or linked
- One line folding in the standing invite: "Want feedback on your number? Join the free community: {community_link}."

## Gate

Email only. No phone number; this is a top-of-funnel, decision-adjacent resource where phone number would cost more conversion than it's worth qualifying for.

## Measurement plan

Track landing-page conversion rate (target 25 to 45 percent per `references/benchmarks.md`, template format) and whether people who download it book a call within 14 days at a normal rate for the account.
