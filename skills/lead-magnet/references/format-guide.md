# Format build notes

Detail per format referenced from SKILL.md step 1.

## Checklist

Best for process steps and quick wins. One to two pages, numbered or boxed items, grouped into sections past 10 items. Use actionable language ("confirm X", "set up Y"). Order by the sequence the reader does the work in.

## Cheat sheet

Best for reference material someone looks up rather than reads start to finish. One page, two at most. Dense but scannable: tables, grids, bolded key items. Design it to be saved or printed, not read once and discarded.

## Template or spreadsheet

Best for repeatable work. Must be usable within 5 minutes of opening: pre-filled example data, a short "how to use" section, placeholder brackets for anything the reader customizes. A spreadsheet needs its formula cells protected and its input cells obvious.

## Swipe file

A curated set of 15 to 50 examples, each with a short note on why it works and how to adapt it. The curation and annotation is the value, not the raw collection. Credit sources.

## Mini-course (email or short video series)

3 to 5 lessons delivered every 1 to 2 days. Each lesson stands alone: if someone only opens lesson 2, they should still learn something usable. Teach, then give one small exercise.

## Quiz or assessment

5 to 10 multiple-choice questions (7 is the sweet spot), 3 to 5 result categories, each with a short personalized recommendation. The quiz itself can be ungated; gate the results behind an email. See **quiz-funnel** for the full scored-diagnostic method, including routing by weakest area.

## Webinar or workshop

30 to 45 minutes of real teaching plus 15 minutes of questions. Promote 1 to 2 weeks out, send a confirmation plus two reminders. Record it; the replay extends the life of the asset. A webinar that is only a pitch damages trust and should not be built.
