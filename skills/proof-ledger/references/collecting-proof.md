# Collecting proof

Scripts and checklists for getting results and permission in writing. Linked from the proof-ledger SKILL.md.

## When to ask

Ask at the moment the result is visible, not months later:

- A client sends a happy message. Reply the same day.
- A milestone date passes (end of program, 90 days in, a job finished).
- A review lands. Thank them, then ask whether you may quote it elsewhere.

## Permission request (text or email)

> Hi [first name], thank you for this. Would you be OK with us sharing what you said, and the result, with other [type of customer] who are where you were? You can pick:
> 1. With your name (and photo, if you like)
> 2. Without your name or anything that identifies you
> 3. Not at all, that's completely fine
>
> We'd use it on [website / sales calls / social posts / ads]. Nothing changes about how we work with you whatever you pick.

Record the reply as the source of the permission. If they pick 1 for some channels only, write the scope in the Permission cell.

Never offer a discount, gift or credit in exchange for a testimonial or review.

## The four questions for a result

1. What was it like before? (their words, as quoted)
2. What changed, by how much, between which dates?
3. What did you do on your side to get there?
4. Name, no name, or not at all?

## Receipts that count

| Claim type | Good receipt | Not enough |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue or sales | Export from the accounting, payment or booking system with the date range visible | A number said on a call |
| Leads or bookings | CRM or calendar export with dates | A bot dashboard number, or a tag count |
| Testimonial | The original message or a signed form, dated | A paraphrase you wrote |
| Review | The public URL plus a screenshot, dated | "We have lots of 5-star reviews" |
| Credential | License number, certificate, registration | A bio line |
| Media | Link to the episode, article or listing | "I've been on podcasts" |

## Anonymizing checklist

- [ ] No name, initials of a rare name, face, voice, handle, company name or logo
- [ ] Location no smaller than a metro area
- [ ] Dates no finer than month or quarter
- [ ] No rare job title, family detail or event that points to one person
- [ ] File names, alt text and captions contain none of the above
- [ ] Health business: written consent on file under the practice's privacy process, or the story isn't used

## Wording checks for a result

- [ ] States the base and the window
- [ ] Says total or caused-by-us
- [ ] If it's a standout result, states what clients typically get, from real data (a "results vary" line alone isn't enough)
- [ ] Uses two facts (before, after) rather than a big percent
- [ ] Exact wording is no stronger than the receipt
