# Offer publish gate

Run every line before the offer goes on a page, in an ad or into a script. One failed line means it doesn't ship. Fix, then rerun.

## The gate

**Economics**
- [ ] Every delivery cost is listed or marked unknown (never zero)
- [ ] Gross margin after delivery and commission is 45% or more (target 60%)
- [ ] The first step doesn't lose money on its own
- [ ] Discount over 30% of normal value is backed by added value of at least 15%, or removed
- [ ] Every obliged session, visit or hour fits the calendar for the volume you plan to sell

**Value**
- [ ] Every "value" figure is your own published price or says "not sold separately"
- [ ] A "worth $X" total appears only if every line has a confirmed basis
- [ ] No credit, coupon, gift card or percent-off counted as value
- [ ] At least one thing the buyer keeps that isn't money off
- [ ] No item exists only to inflate the stack

**Promises**
- [ ] The guarantee covers only what the business controls, with written conditions
- [ ] Scarcity has a real count, a real close date and a reason, or there is none
- [ ] The name doesn't promise permanence, a measured result, a guarantee or a cure, and uses no brand you don't own
- [ ] Every claim maps to an approved proof-ledger row or a visible [NEEDS PROOF]

**Terms**
- [ ] What's included and excluded is written
- [ ] When payment is taken, cancellation, reschedule and refund terms are written
- [ ] Any credit or deposit: applied once to the balance, with minimum spend, expiry and limit written
- [ ] Any financing shows the example amount, term and annual rate together, or isn't shown

**One version**
- [ ] The paste-everywhere block exists
- [ ] Site, ads, scripts, booking page and invoice all match it (list each surface checked)

**Health businesses only**
- [ ] The clinical lead approved what the first visit is and what it may conclude
- [ ] The offer says treatment is separate, not required and decided after assessment
- [ ] **health-claims-check** passed

## Example Offer Sheet (fictional)

Northgate Coaching and every number below are invented to show the format.

```markdown
# Offer Sheet: The Monday Numbers Program
Version: 2 · Approved by: owner · Date: 2026-10-11

**Promise:** We help service-business owners doing $20K to $80K a month know exactly
which number to fix each week, so they grow without guessing, without hiring a
marketing team.
**For:** owners who make the marketing decisions and will share their numbers weekly.
**Not for:** owners who want it done for them; anyone who won't share CRM access.
**First step:** The Numbers Review, $300. A 60-minute review of your last 90 days that
ends with your weakest stage named and one fix. Credited once to the program if you
join within 14 days. If the review shows the program isn't a fit, we'll say so.

## What's included
| Item | What it does for you | Value basis |
|---|---|---|
| 12 weekly group calls | Fix one number each week with the group | not sold separately |
| Monthly 1:1 numbers review (3) | Your plan, adjusted to your data | $300 each (published) |
| Monday Numbers Template | Your weekly scoreboard, filled in 15 minutes | not sold separately |
| Setter script and follow-up library | Stops leads going cold | not sold separately |
| Message access, weekdays | Answers within one business day | not sold separately |

**Price:** $3,600, or 3 payments of $1,250 (total $3,750)
**Margin:** delivery cost $1,180, price $3,600, gross margin 67% (internal only)
**Guarantee:** After 30 days, if the program isn't useful, tell us and we refund what
you've paid, no argument.
**Scarcity:** 12 places per cohort, because the group calls work best at 12 or fewer.
Next cohort closes 2026-11-01.
**Terms:** payment at enrollment; 30-day refund as above; reschedule a 1:1 with 24 hours'
notice; review credit applied once, within 14 days, not combinable.
**Capacity:** 12 per cohort, 3 cohorts a year.

## Paste-everywhere block
The Monday Numbers Program. 12 weeks. $3,600 or 3 payments of $1,250.
Weekly group calls, 3 one-to-one reviews, the Monday Numbers Template and the setter library.
30-day refund if it isn't useful. 12 places per cohort.

Proof used: none yet. [NEEDS PROOF: a client result on weekly revenue or leads]
```
