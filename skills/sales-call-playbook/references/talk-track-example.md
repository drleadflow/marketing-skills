# Example talk track: a fictional business coach

Northgate Coaching, the owner, the prospect and every number are invented. The offer is the fictional Monday Numbers Program from the offer-builder example: 12 weeks, $3,600 or 3 payments of $1,250, 30-day refund.

## Pre-call brief

- Booked for: The Numbers Review follow-up call · Source: Instagram carousel, then the free scoreboard template
- What they said on the form: "I'm busy every week but the bank account doesn't show it."
- Told so far: the call is 45 minutes; the program exists; no price given
- Qualifying answers: owner, 4 staff, about $40K a month, runs Facebook ads through an agency
- Open concern: "I've done a course before and never finished it."

## The call

**Open**
> "Thanks for making the time. Here's my plan: I'll ask what you're trying to do and what's in the way. If I think the program fits, I'll tell you how it works and what it costs. If it doesn't, I'll tell you that too, and point you somewhere better. Fair?"

**Goal**
> "You wrote that you're busy every week but the bank account doesn't show it. Tell me more about that."
> "If this went well, what would be different a year from now?"
> Prospect: "I'd know where the money's going. And I'd take a Friday off without my phone."
> "Why does the Friday matter?"

**Now**
> "What have you tried?"
> Prospect: "An agency for ads. A course on funnels. I didn't finish it."
> "What happened with the agency?"
> Prospect: "Leads came in. I don't know what happened to them."
> "If nothing changes for six months, what does that cost you?"
> Prospect: "Probably another agency. And my wife's patience."

**Priority**
> "Let me make sure I've got this. You want to know where the money's going and take a Friday off. What's in the way is that leads come in and you can't see what happens to them. You tried an agency and a course, and the course didn't stick because you were on your own with it. Is that right?"
> Prospect: "Yeah. That's exactly it."

**Recommend**
> "Then I'd recommend the Monday Numbers Program. It fits what you said for two reasons. First, every week you look at the same few numbers, so you'll see exactly where leads stop. Second, you're not on your own: there's a group call every week and a one-to-one review every month, so it's hard to drift like the course. It works in three parts: we set up your scoreboard in week one, we find the weakest number, and each week you fix one thing. What it won't do is run your ads for you. You or your agency still do that."
> (Stop talking.)

**Price and terms**
> "It's $3,600 for the 12 weeks, or three payments of $1,250, which comes to $3,750. That includes the 12 group calls, three one-to-one reviews, the template and the setter scripts. If after 30 days it isn't useful, you tell us and we refund what you've paid. The next group starts November 3."

**Concerns**
> "What would stop you from going ahead?"
> Prospect: "Honestly, finishing. I didn't finish the last one."
> "That's a fair worry. Here's what's different: you're on a call with 11 other owners every Monday, and I'll see your numbers every week. If you miss a week, someone notices. And if you're not using it after 30 days, you get your money back. Does that answer it?"
> Prospect: "It does."

**Decision**
> "Would you like to go ahead?"
> Prospect: "Yes. Three payments."
> "Great. I'll send the payment link now while we're on. [Payment taken.] Your onboarding is booked for Tuesday at 10. What's the first thing you'll do this week?"
> Prospect: "Get you access to the ad account and the CRM."
