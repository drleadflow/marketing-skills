# Worked example: call grade (fictional business coach discovery call)

Transcript excerpt (fictional, Avery Lane Coaching): rep opened well, skipped most of Goal and Now, pitched the $1,000/month program within 4 minutes without a Priority playback, prospect said "let me think about it," rep said "sounds good, just let me know" and ended the call with no date.

## Call Grade: 3.0 minus 2 = 1.0/10

| # | Stage | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open | 7/10 | Set the agenda clearly, referenced the lead source. Didn't confirm "not a fit" was a safe answer. |
| 2 | Goal | 2/10 | Only one question asked before moving on. No goal captured in the prospect's own words. |
| 3 | Now | 1/10 | Never asked what they'd already tried or what waiting is costing them. |
| 4 | Priority | 0/10 | Skipped entirely. No playback, no "that's right" moment. |
| 5 | Recommend | 4/10 | Gave the standard pitch, not connected to anything the prospect said, since nothing specific was gathered. |
| 6 | Price and terms | 5/10 | Stated the price once, but didn't walk through terms or payment options. |
| 7 | Concerns | 3/10 | Prospect's "think about it" was treated as closed, not explored for what's unresolved. |
| 8 | Decision | 2/10 | Never asked directly for the decision; ended on "let me know whenever," no date. |

Stage average: (7+2+1+0+4+5+3+2) / 8 = 24 / 8 = **3.0**

### Hard-deduction flags
1. Recommendation given before Priority got an explicit "that's right" (Priority was skipped entirely) (1 point off)
2. Call ended with no specific next step (no date, no booked follow-up) (1 point off)

2 flags, 2 points off (under the 3-point cap).

**Final score: 3.0 minus 2 = 1.0/10**

### Coaching fixes (ranked by impact)
1. **Priority stage skipped entirely**
 - Transcript: "So, cool, sounds like you're ready to grow. Let me tell you about the program."
 - Why it hurts: Jumps straight from a thin Goal answer to the pitch, with no "let me make sure I've got this" playback. The recommendation has nothing real to connect to.
 - Fix: "Let me make sure I've got this. You want [goal, their words], and what's gotten in the way is [obstacle, their words]. Is that right?" Wait for the explicit "that's right" before recommending anything.

2. **No specific next step**
 - Transcript: "Sounds good, just let me know whenever."
 - Why it hurts: Leaves the follow-up entirely on the prospect, who is now far more likely to never reply.
 - Fix: "Totally fine to think it over. Can I follow up Thursday at 2pm to see where you landed?"

3. **No direct ask for the decision**
 - Transcript: [call moved straight from price to "let me know"]
 - Why it hurts: The call never asks for a yes, so "think about it" never gets tested for what's specifically unresolved.
 - Fix: "So where does that leave you? Do you want to move forward?"

### Verdict
Strong opener, but Goal, Now, and Priority are all being skipped in favor of pitching early, and the call ends with no real next step. Fix Priority first (the explicit playback and "that's right"); Recommend and Decision will both improve once there's something real to connect to.
