# Keyword example, end to end

A full worked example for a fictional business coach, Clearhead Coaching, promoting a lead magnet called "Price Your First Offer" (a one-page worksheet, see **lead-magnet**). Swap the keyword, resource, and voice for a different business; keep the structure.

## Keyword

`PRICE`

## Post caption (excerpt)

> "...if you're not sure what to charge for your first offer, comment PRICE and I'll send you the worksheet I use with every new client."

## Public comment reply (rotate 3 to 5 of these so it doesn't read as identical every time)

1. "Sending it now, check your DMs!"
2. "On its way, {commenter_name}!"
3. "Just sent you a DM with it!"
4. "Heading to your inbox now!"
5. "Sent! Let me know if it doesn't show up."

## DM sequence

**Opening DM (one button, title: "Send me the worksheet")**
> Hey! Here's the Price Your First Offer worksheet. Tap below and I'll get it over to you.

**After button tap**
> What's your best email? I'll send a copy there too.

**If a valid email is given**
> Perfect, here's the worksheet: {resource_link}. Also just sent a copy to your inbox.

**If no valid email after one retry**
> No worries, here it is anyway: {resource_link}.

## Resource page outline (what {resource_link} opens to)

1. **Headline**: "Price Your First Offer"
2. **One-line framing**: what the worksheet helps someone figure out in the next 10 minutes
3. **The worksheet itself**: the actual content, consumable without needing anything else
4. **One line at the bottom**: the standing community invite, folded in here rather than as a second DM link, for example: "Want more of this? Join the free community: {community_link}."

## Follow-up DM

Meta bars automated promotional messages to someone outside the 24-hour window after their last message in the thread (see **messaging-compliance**). A delivery DM they never replied to does not reopen that window.

- **Inside 24 hours of their last message**: an automated follow-up is fine. "Hey, did you get a chance to run through the worksheet? Happy to answer anything if a number felt off."
- **Past 24 hours**: only a human sends the follow-up, or the platform's human-agent tag is used where the account is eligible for it. Never an automated send past the window, even a soft one.

Either way, this follow-up routes to **setter-script** if it turns into a real conversation about booking time, and stops entirely if there's no reply after one attempt.

## Rules this example follows

- One keyword, one resource, one trigger (see SKILL.md step 1).
- Exactly one link in the delivery DM; the community invite lives inside the resource page, not as a second DM link (see SKILL.md step 4).
- Resource delivers even without an email (see SKILL.md step 3).
