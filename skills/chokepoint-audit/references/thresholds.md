# Chokepoint thresholds

Starting lines for scoring each chokepoint red, amber or green. They come from running service businesses (coaches, agencies, local services, health practices), not from a study. Use them until the owner has 6 or more months of their own data, then score against their own best quarter and say so in the report.

Every rate: numerator, denominator, window. Every blank: "not recorded".

## Canonical benchmarks (every skill in this repo points here)

These five are the shared starting points. Other skills (marketing-scoreboard, show-rate, setter-script, speed-to-lead, nurture-cadence, repeat-purchase and the rest) link to this table instead of keeping their own numbers. Each is a starting point: replace it with the business's own best quarter once it has 6 months of data.

| Metric | Numerator ÷ denominator, same window | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speed to first reply, calls and forms, business hours | Median minutes from lead created to first human or real conversational reply (auto-acknowledgments don't count) | 5 min or less | 6 to 60 min | over 60 min |
| Speed to first reply, social DMs | Same, for DMs | 15 min or less | 16 to 120 min | over 120 min |
| Lead to booked, cold leads (paid social, cold outreach) | Booked appointments on the calendar ÷ leads (people, not messages) | 20% or more | 10 to 19% | under 10% |
| Lead to booked, warm leads (referral, inbound call, search) | Same | 40% or more | 25 to 39% | under 25% |
| Show rate | Showed ÷ booked appointments whose date has passed | 75% or more | 60 to 74% | under 60% |
| Close rate | Bought ÷ showed | 40% or more (flag above 65%) | 25 to 39% | under 25% |
| Value multiple | Revenue per customer over their first 12 months ÷ average first purchase | 4x or more | 2 to 3.9x | under 2x |

Notes:
- **Close rate above 65%** is green, with a flag: the price may be low or the calls may be screening too hard. Check with **offer-ladder-pricing**.
- **Value multiple** doesn't apply to one-time purchases, including one-time high-ticket programs (a single coaching program, a roof, a wedding). Score those businesses on Referrals and past-customer contact instead.
- **Cost per booked appointment** = paid ad spend ÷ bookings from paid sources. Never divide paid-only spend by bookings from all sources.
- **Lead source unknown**: use the cold-lead line, and add a source field.

## 1. Offer

| Metric | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gross margin on the main offer, after delivery cost and commission | 60% or more | 45 to 59% | under 45% |
| First-purchase contribution vs full cost to acquire a customer | covers it | covers 50 to 99%, and repeat buying is proven | under 50%, or repeat buying not proven |
| Price and terms identical on site, ads, scripts, invoices | yes | | no (any conflict is red) |
| Share of lost sales where the stated reason is price or "not sure it's for me" | under 30% | 30 to 50% | over 50% |

Delivery cost includes materials, the time of whoever delivers it at full employment cost, and processing fees. Count each cost once. If the owner doesn't know delivery cost, Offer is **not recorded**, and that's the first thing to measure.

## 2. Attention

| Metric | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leads per month ÷ leads the goal needs (backward math) | 100% or more | 70 to 99% | under 70% |
| Cost per lead ÷ most you can pay per lead | 1.0 or less | 1.0 to 1.5 | over 1.5 |
| Weeks in the last 8 with at least one piece of content or ad running | 8 | 5 to 7 | 4 or fewer |

Only score Attention as the chokepoint when every later stage is green or amber.

## 3. Leads

| Metric | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median minutes to first real reply, business hours (calls, forms) | 5 or less | 6 to 60 | over 60 |
| Median minutes to first reply, social DMs | 15 or less | 16 to 120 | over 120 |
| After-hours leads: first human touch after the next shift starts | within 30 min | within 2 hours | later, or never |
| Share of leads who got any real reply or call | 95% or more | 80 to 94% | under 80% |
| Page or post visitors who become leads (cold paid traffic) | 10% or more | 5 to 9% | under 5% |

An automatic "thanks, we got your message" is not a reply. Measure coverage before coaching speed: if a third of leads never get called, calling the rest faster won't fix it.

## 4. Appointments

| Metric | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead to booked, cold leads (paid social, cold outreach) | 20% or more | 10 to 19% | under 10% |
| Lead to booked, warm leads (referrals, inbound calls, search) | 40% or more | 25 to 39% | under 25% |
| Show rate (showed ÷ booked with past date) | 75% or more | 60 to 74% | under 60% |
| Booking-ready replies that got two real time options | 90% or more | 60 to 89% | under 60% |

If shows aren't recorded on the calendar, Appointments is **not recorded** and fixing that is the first action. Count cancelled, rescheduled, missed and still-scheduled separately.

## 5. Closed

| Metric | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Close rate (bought ÷ showed) on a sales call or consult | 40% or more | 25 to 39% | under 25% |
| Undecided prospects with a dated next step and an owner | 90% or more | 60 to 89% | under 60% |
| Average first purchase, quarter over quarter | rising or flat | down under 10% | down 10% or more |

For businesses where the customer buys at booking (a fixed-price service), close rate is near 100% by design; score Closed on average first purchase and on the share of quotes accepted instead. Track same-day and later purchases separately and give each group its full decision window.

## 6. Fulfillment

| Metric | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Refunds plus complaints, share of customers in the window | 2% or less | 3 to 5% | over 5% |
| Customers who finished what they bought (program, plan, project) | 80% or more | 60 to 79% | under 60% |
| Customers who hit the first promised milestone on time | 80% or more | 60 to 79% | under 60% |

If none of these are tracked, ask the one-question check: "Out of your last 10 customers, how many would you bet would buy again?" Under 6 is a red flag worth investigating, labeled suspected.

## 7. Retention

| Metric | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12-month customer value ÷ average first purchase | 4x or more | 2 to 3.9x | under 2x |
| Customers who bought again within the normal return window | 50% or more | 30 to 49% | under 30% |
| Monthly cancellation, recurring services | 3% or less | 4 to 6% | over 6% |
| Past customers contacted with something useful in the last 90 days | yes | | no |

For one-time purchases (a roof, a wedding, a single high-ticket program), skip the value multiple and score Retention on the past-customer contact line and lean on Referrals.

## 8. Referrals

| Metric | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| New customers who came from a referral | 25% or more | 10 to 24% | under 10% |
| New reviews ÷ new customers | 20% or more | 10 to 19% | under 10% |
| The referral ask is a named step at a set moment, done every time | yes | sometimes | no |

If referral source isn't asked at booking, Referrals is **not recorded**. Add "How did you hear about us?" to every booking.

## Leak math, by chokepoint

Hold every other rate where it is. Label all results modeled, not a forecast.

| Chokepoint | Extra new customers per month |
|---|---|
| Offer | Usually not modeled as customers. Model margin: (green margin − current margin) × monthly revenue |
| Attention | (leads needed − leads now) × lead to booked × show × close |
| Leads | leads with no real reply × booking rate of reached leads × show × close |
| Appointments (booking) | leads × (green booking rate − current) × show × close |
| Appointments (show) | bookings × (green show rate − current) × close |
| Closed | shows × (green close rate − current) |
| Fulfillment | Model refunds avoided: (current rate − 2%) × customers × average refund |
| Retention | customers × (green repeat rate − current) × average repeat purchase, as revenue |
| Referrals | new customers × (green referral share − current), as extra customers |

Multiply extra customers by average first purchase for revenue. Never add expected later revenue on top.
