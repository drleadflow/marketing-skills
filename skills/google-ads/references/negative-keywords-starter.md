# Negative keyword starter list and worked example

This is a starting point to adapt per business, not a universal list to paste in unchanged.

## Common negatives worth checking for most local service businesses

- "free," "cheap," "DIY," "how to do it yourself"
- "jobs," "careers," "salary," "hiring," "internship"
- "course," "training," "certification," "school" (unless the business sells education)
- "reviews," "complaints," "lawsuit," "scam" (research-intent, not buyer-intent, usually)
- "wholesale," "supplier," "for sale" (if the business sells services, not equipment)
- Competitor brand names, unless the business owner has explicitly decided to bid on them

## Fictional worked example

Fictional business "Cedarbrook Family Chiropractic." After the first two weeks of the weekly
search-terms review:

| Search term that triggered an ad | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "chiropractic assistant jobs" | Added as negative | Job seeker, not a patient |
| "chiropractor salary" | Added as negative | Research, not a buyer |
| "best chiropractor cedarbrook" | Promoted to its own exact-match keyword | Recurring, clearly high-intent, performing well as a phrase match |
| "chiropractor insurance billing question" | Added as negative | Existing-patient support question, not a new-patient search |

Reviewing weekly instead of monthly caught the first two before they'd spent more than a few
dollars each; left for a month, low-relevance terms like these can quietly absorb a meaningful
share of a small daily budget.
