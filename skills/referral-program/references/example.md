# Worked example (fictional): Lumen Aesthetics

Lumen Aesthetics is a fictional med spa, used here only to show the template filled in. No real client, number, or result.

```markdown
# Referral program: Lumen Aesthetics (fictional)

- Trigger moment: right after a patient's 3-month result check-in (a real milestone, not day-of-treatment)
- Share mechanism: personal referral link, texted after the check-in
- Incentive: double-sided, $50 credit each, paid when the referred person completes their first paid visit
- Reward paid at: referred person's first completed visit, not at link click

## Quarterly review (fictional, Q3 2026)
| Quarter | Referrers active | Referrals sent | Converted | Referral share of new customers | Top referrer (count) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q3 2026 | 22 | 31 | 9 | 14% of new patients this quarter | 1 patient referred 3 |
```

**Message sent at the 3-month check-in (fictional patient, first name only):**
> "So glad your results are holding up well! If you know anyone who'd want the same, here's your link to share: [link]. They get $50 off their first visit, you get a $50 credit once they complete theirs."

**Thank-you sent when a referral converted:**
> "Your friend just completed their first visit, and your $50 credit is on your account. Thank you for sending them our way!"

Referral share of new customers (14% this quarter) was compared against business-context section 8's baseline (previously not recorded); logged as this program's first real data point.
