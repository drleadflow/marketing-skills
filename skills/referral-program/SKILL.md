# Referral Program

You'll finish with a referral program with a real trigger moment, real message copy, and an incentive structure sized to this business's actual numbers.

## Before you start

Read `.agents/business-context.md` (section 8: Numbers, for "Average order value, repeat purchases" and cost per lead) and **messaging-compliance** if they exist. If business-context is missing, offer to run **business-context** first, or ask only what's missing:
1. Do customers already refer people informally, even without a program?
2. What reward would feel worth sharing to this business's own customers, not a generic "$10 off"?
3. Is there a simple way to give someone a shareable link or code today, or does this start as a manual process?

## How to do it

1. **Pick the trigger moment**: right after a win, a milestone, exceptional service, or a renewal.
2. **Make the ask itself shareable**: a personal link or code, a pre-written message, or a physical card.
3. **Choose the incentive structure**: single-sided (simpler, fits high-value purchases), double-sided (converts better), or tiered. Lead with the dollar amount, not a percentage, on lower-priced items.
4. **Reward at the real milestone**, not at a click.
5. **Give the referrer something to share**, never the friend's contact details.
6. **Record the referral source at booking**, under your privacy rules, and never report the new customer's spending back to the referrer.
7. **Review quarterly** using the template below.
8. **If there's no referral software yet, track it manually first.** A shared spreadsheet with referrer name, code or link given, date given, and date converted is a real program; don't wait on a tool purchase to start asking.

## Message templates

**The ask (sent at the trigger moment, e.g. right after a strong result or milestone):**
> "So glad [specific result/milestone] worked out! If you know anyone who'd want the same, here's your link to share: [link]. They get [their reward], you get [your reward] once they [milestone, e.g. complete their first visit]."

**Reminder, 30 days after the ask if unused (one follow-up only):**
> "Just a reminder, your referral link is still live: [link]. No pressure, just didn't want you to miss it if someone comes to mind."

**Thank-you, sent when a referral converts:**
> "Your referral [first name only, if appropriate] just [became a customer / completed their first visit], and your [reward] is on its way. Thank you!"

## Template: referral program sheet

```markdown
# Referral program: [Business]

- Trigger moment: [right after X]
- Share mechanism: [link / code / card]
- Incentive: [single-sided $x / double-sided $x each / tiered]
- Reward paid at: [milestone, not click]

## Quarterly review
| Quarter | Referrers active | Referrals sent | Converted | Referral share of new customers | Top referrer (count) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Referral share of new customers is tracked in business-context section 8; compare this program's number against that baseline each quarter.
```

## Check it

- [ ] The ask happens at a real win or milestone, not at signup before there's anything to say
- [ ] The referrer has something concrete and easy to share
- [ ] The reward pays out at a real milestone, not at a click
- [ ] No step asks the referrer to hand over a friend's contact details directly
- [ ] Referral source is recorded under your privacy rules, never reported back with the new customer's spending

## Rules

- Never ask the customer to hand over a friend's contact information; give them something to share instead.
- Never report a referred customer's purchases or treatment details back to the person who referred them.
- If this is a health or legal-adjacent business, check any financial reward against platform and regulatory rules for your services and location before launching it.
- Don't invent a reward's retail value just to make the offer sound bigger.
- Never send the referral ask or its reminder by text without the consent messaging-compliance requires for a marketing-style message.
