# Voice note scripts

Three scripts, each written to land at roughly 30 to 45 seconds spoken aloud (about 75 to 110 words at a normal conversational pace). Read one aloud before recording; if it rushes past 45 seconds, cut a sentence rather than speeding up the delivery. Fictional businesses: Clearhead Coaching, Ridgeline HVAC, Willow Aesthetics. `{rep_name}` below is a human setter's real name. If an AI setter is voicing these instead, swap in a disclosed assistant persona (see **ai-setter-guardrails**) and be ready to disclose being an AI if asked, the same as in text.

## 1. Personal thank-you after a first step (Clearhead Coaching, 38 seconds / ~90 words)

> "Hey {first_name}, it's {rep_name}. Just wanted to send a quick voice note instead of another text, saw you grabbed the offer audit and wanted to say thanks for taking the time. I know it's a small thing to book 20 minutes, but it's usually the step people put off the longest. Looking forward to Thursday, and if anything comes up before then, just reply here, I'll see it. Talk soon."

## 2. Warm re-engagement after a few quiet days (Ridgeline HVAC, 33 seconds / ~80 words)

> "Hi {first_name}, it's {rep_name} from Ridgeline. Haven't heard back since we talked about the {issue} at {address}, figured a voice note might be easier than another text. No pressure at all if the timing's just not right, I just wanted to check in as a real person instead of a script. If you want to grab a window this week, text me back and I'll get you on the calendar."

## 3. Answering a hesitant "I'm not sure" with tone (Willow Aesthetics, 42 seconds / ~105 words)

> "Hey {first_name}, it's {rep_name} from Willow. Totally hear you on wanting to think it over, that's a completely normal reaction and I'd rather you take your time than feel rushed into anything. The consult itself doesn't commit you to treatment either way, it's really just a conversation with the provider about what's realistic for what you're looking at. If it helps, I'm happy to answer any specific questions over text first, no pressure to book anything today. Just let me know."

## Production notes

- Script first, record second, even for a live human reading it; don't improvise from a bullet list.
- If the business's identity decision is "presents as the owner," record in that person's real voice; if "presents as an assistant," the script above already reflects that framing and should not claim to be the owner.
- Any cloned or synthetic voice used to produce these requires the real speaker's documented consent before use; see SKILL.md rule 4.
- Always pair the voice note with a short text restating the concrete next step (a time, a link) in the same thread; never put a bookable detail only in audio.
