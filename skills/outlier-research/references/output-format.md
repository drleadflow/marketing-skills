# Outlier research output format

```
## Outlier Research: [account/topic] on [platform]
Window: [date range] | Baseline: [median metric over N posts]

### Outlier #1: ratio [X.Xx]
- Metric: [views/likes/saves] [N] vs baseline [N]
- Hook type: [category]
- Hook (paraphrased, not quoted verbatim if from a third party): [shape]
- Body structure: [shape]
- CTA: [shape]
- Format: [talking-head/carousel/B-roll/text]
- Length: [time/words]
- Why it worked: [1-2 sentences]
- Repeatable? [yes/no: if no, say why: trending audio, one-off news, creator-specific authority]

### Outlier #2: ratio [X.Xx]
[same structure]

### Outlier #3: ratio [X.Xx]
[same structure]

### Reusable templates extracted
1. [Template name]: [one-line shape]: fits pillar: [pillar name from content-pillars]
2. [Template name]: [one-line shape]: fits pillar: [pillar name]

### Recommendation
[Which 1-2 templates to test first, and why]
```

## Fictional worked example: home-services company

```
## Outlier Research: Lindqvist Heating & Air (fictional) on Instagram Reels
Window: last 90 days | Baseline: 1,200 views over last 25 reels

### Outlier #1: ratio 14.2x
- Metric: 17,000 views vs baseline 1,200
- Hook type: Negative frame / mistake callout
- Hook shape: "[Common action] is costing you [specific cost]."
- Body structure: Named mistake, mechanism of why it happens, one-sentence fix
- CTA: "Comment 'QUOTE' and we'll check your system for free"
- Format: Talking-head, tech on camera in the field
- Length: 42 seconds
- Why it worked: Loss aversion plus a real-looking field setting (not a studio) made it feel like insider access.
- Repeatable? Yes. Format doesn't depend on a trend, just a real job site and a specific mistake.

### Reusable templates extracted
1. "Field Mistake Callout": tech names a mistake on a real job, shows the fix, CTA to a free check: fits pillar: "What Contractors Won't Tell You"

### Recommendation
Test "Field Mistake Callout" twice this week before investing in a second template; it's the clearest repeatable win and matches an existing pillar.
```
