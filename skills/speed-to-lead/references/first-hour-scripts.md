# First-hour scripts

Fill-in drafts for the 3-touch first-hour sequence in SKILL.md step 2. Fictional businesses throughout: Clearhead Coaching (business coach), Ridgeline HVAC (home services), Willow Aesthetics (med spa). Replace every `{blank}` before sending.

## Clearhead Coaching (business coach)

**Touch 1, SMS, within 5 minutes**
> Hey {first_name}, this is {rep_name} from Clearhead, got your offer audit request. Quick one before I block time for you: what's the main thing you're trying to fix right now, pricing, leads, or follow-up?

**Touch 2, call attempt + voicemail, within 2 hours (if no answer)**
> "Hey {first_name}, it's {rep_name} from Clearhead, following up on the offer audit request you put in. No worries if now's not a good time, I'll shoot you a text too. Talk soon."

**Touch 3, email, same evening**
> Subject: the thing that fixed {similar_result_niche}'s pricing problem
>
> {first_name}, saw your audit request come through earlier. While you're thinking it over, here's a short breakdown of how {proof_example} fixed the exact thing you flagged: {link_or_attachment}. Want to grab 20 minutes this week? {booking_link}

## Ridgeline HVAC (home services)

>
> {first_name}, we've got your request on file for {address}. Next available diagnostic window is {date/time}. Reply to confirm or let us know a better time. {booking_link}

## Concern-routed quick replies

Use these when a reply to any of the three touches above is a concern rather than a question. Stop the scheduled cascade and answer with the matched reply, not a repeat of the script.

**"Not the right time"**
> Totally understand, {first_name}. No pressure at all. Want me to check back in a couple weeks, or would you rather reach out whenever it's a better fit?

**"Too expensive" / price objection**
> Appreciate you being upfront, {first_name}. Before we close this out, can I ask what budget would feel comfortable? Might be a version of this that fits, might not, happy to just tell you honestly either way.

Both close with a real question, never a repeated pitch, and both log the person's actual answer rather than guessing at it. See **follow-up-sequence** references for the day 2 to 7 version of these same two concerns.
