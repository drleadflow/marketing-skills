# Worked example: GBP setup and local page (fictional home-services company)

Business: Lindqvist Heating & Air (fictional). Service-area business, no public storefront address. Serves 4 named suburbs.

## GBP profile foundation

- Name: "Lindqvist Heating & Air" (matches the website footer and every invoice, no extra keywords added)
- Address: hidden, service area set to the 4 served suburbs plus their zip codes
- Primary category: HVAC Contractor. Secondary: Air Conditioning Repair Service, Heating Contractor
- Hours: Mon-Fri 7am-6pm, Sat 8am-2pm, closed Sun, with holiday hours updated each season
- Phone: direct line, answered by the dispatch team during posted hours
- Website: links to the HVAC maintenance plan page, not the homepage, since that's the most relevant next step for a searcher

## Services filled out

- Furnace repair, with a one-line description of typical response time
- AC installation, with a one-line description of what's included in a quote
- Seasonal maintenance plans, with a one-line description of the twice-yearly visit cadence

## Weekly post (tip type, from the templates)

```
"Is it normal for my furnace to click before it starts?"
A single click on startup is usually the igniter, totally normal. A repeated clicking
that doesn't catch is usually a failed igniter or a gas valve issue, worth a call
before it fails on the coldest night of the year.
```

## Review velocity plan

Every completed job gets a review request text 2 hours after the technician leaves, the same message every time, no incentive attached. Negative reviews get a response within 24 hours offering to make it right, without confirming any private details of the job in public.

## Real customer questions, answered on the website (Google removed the public GBP Q&A box in December 2025, so these live on the site's FAQ section instead, where Maps' AI answer feature can draw from them)

1. "Do you charge for the first visit?" - answered with the real diagnostic fee.
2. "Do you work on weekends?" - answered with real Saturday hours.
3. "How fast can someone come out?" - answered with a real, honest response-time range.

## Citations/NAP check

Audited: Google Business Profile, website footer, Yelp, Angi, and the local Chamber of Commerce directory. Found: Yelp still showed the old phone number from before a 2024 number change. Fixed.
