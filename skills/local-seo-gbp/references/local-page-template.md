# Local landing page template (one per service area)

```
# [Service] in [City/Neighborhood]

[Answer-first opening: 2-3 sentences stating directly what the business does in this
area and for whom. Follows seo-article's answer-first rule.]

## Why [City/Neighborhood] customers choose [business name]

[2-4 sentences specific to this area: what's different about serving it, real local
detail (climate, housing stock, permit rules, drive time from the business's base),
not a copy-paste paragraph with only the city name changed.]

## What we handle in [City/Neighborhood]

- [Service 1, with a one-line local detail if one exists]
- [Service 2]
- [Service 3]

## [Local proof point, if cleared in the proof ledger]

[A real review, a real local job story, or NEEDS PROOF placeholder if nothing is
cleared yet. Never a generic, unverifiable "we've served hundreds in this area"
claim.]

## Service area

[Named neighborhoods/zip codes covered from this page, not a vague
"and surrounding areas."]

## [CTA: book/call/quote]
```

## What makes a local page NOT thin content

- Real area-specific detail beyond the city name: known local issues, typical housing age, permit quirks, drive-time commitment.
- A real local proof point when one exists (with permission cleared), or an honest placeholder instead of a fabricated one.
- A distinct service area list per page, not the same paragraph copied across 20 city pages.

## What to avoid

- Programmatically generating dozens of near-identical pages with only the city name swapped. This reads as thin/duplicate content to both human visitors and ranking systems, and risks a manual action if done at scale.
- Claiming a service area the business can't legally and reliably serve.
