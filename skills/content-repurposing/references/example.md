# Worked example: repurposing one podcast episode (fictional)

Source: a 38-minute podcast episode with Avery Lane Coaching (fictional) and a guest, transcript available.

```
SOURCE: "Why Follow-Up Beats More Leads" podcast ep. 14, 38 min, transcript available

Moment 1 [04:12]: "Most coaches lose deals to a 4-day follow-up gap, not a lead-quality gap."
-> Derivative: Short-form video hook + body (hand to short-form-script)
-> Standalone? Yes, no setup needed.

Moment 2 [11:30]: Guest's 3-step framework for triaging warm leads same-day.
-> Derivative: Carousel, "steps" slide type (hand to carousel-writer)
-> Standalone? Yes.

Moment 3 [22:05]: A specific story about a client who doubled close rate without adding ad spend.
-> Derivative: Newsletter story section (hand to newsletter)
-> Standalone? Needs the client's permission to use the story; mark [NEEDS PROOF: confirm permission] until cleared.

Moment 4 [29:40]: Deep answer to "how do you know when a lead is dead?"
-> Derivative: Blog article section (hand to seo-article)
-> Standalone? Yes.

Release sequence:
Week 1: Moment 1 (video, 9:16, 45 seconds), Moment 2 (carousel, 4:5, 6 slides)
Week 2: Moment 4 (article, no length cap, answers the question fully), Moment 1 repurposed as a text post with a different hook variant
Week 3: Moment 3 (newsletter), once permission is confirmed
```
