# Fixed skeleton template (fictional example)

A fixed skeleton means you write the layout once per offer and reuse it, multiplying by swapping
one variable (area, price shape, person) instead of inventing a new layout for every ad.

## Fictional example: "Fernwood Dental" whitening offer

1. **Local strip**: "NOW AVAILABLE IN <CITY>" small caps bar, top of frame.
2. **Offer name in display type**: "SAME-DAY WHITENING" (the service, never the brand/device name,
   as the headline).
3. **One price shape**: "STARTING AT $149" (never a multi-visit total requiring math).
4. **Benefit bullets (3 to 4)**: outcome words the practice has confirmed it can say,
   pulled from `.agents/proof-ledger.md`. Never universal claims about everyone's results.
5. **Photo**, real staff or real patient with consent on file, annotated only if the annotation is
   accurate (an arrow at the actual treatment area, not a generic glow effect).
6. **CTA pill**: "Book Your Visit."
7. **Address or location bar**: real, current address.

## Per-creative brief built from this skeleton

```
VISUAL SYSTEM: Fernwood skeleton v1 (see above)
IDENTITY: real staff photo, identity-locked, consent on file
LAYOUT (exact on-image text):
  - top strip: "NOW AVAILABLE IN RIVERTON"
  - headline: "SAME-DAY WHITENING"
  - price: "STARTING AT $149"
  - bullets: "One visit." "No trays to take home." "Sensitivity-friendly formula."
  - CTA: "Book Your Visit"
LIGHTING: bright, clinical, natural skin texture
QUALITY BAR: no doll-smooth AI skin, no synthetic sheen
AVOID: no before/after, no device brand name on image, no implied guarantee
```

Multiply by swapping one variable at a time: same skeleton, different area ("Fernwood" chairside
polish vs. take-home kit), different price shape, or a different real staff member, before
inventing a new concept.
