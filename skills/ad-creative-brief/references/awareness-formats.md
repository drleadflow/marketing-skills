# Mapping awareness stage to format

| Stage | What the cell does | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Education or curiosity in the category, no offer | no price on image, soft CTA ("learn more"), own ad set |
| Problem aware | Names the felt problem before the service is on the table | no price, soft CTA, own ad set |
| Solution aware | Why this type of service, mechanism-led | offer card or editorial callout, main ad set |
| Product aware | Why this specific business | social-proof or named-provider format, main ad set |
| Most aware | Retargeting only: what the visit is like, reviews, still-open availability | direct CTA, warm audiences only, never shown cold |

## Steps

1. Lay out the current slate against this table. Count cells per row.
2. Any row with fewer than two cells gets built now. Usually unaware, problem-aware, and
   most-aware are the gaps, because a slate built straight from a landing page naturally stacks in
   the middle three.
3. Unaware and problem-aware cells go into their own prospecting ad set with a soft CTA. Mixed
   into the main offer ad set, they inflate cost per result and get starved of budget.
4. Retargeting cells go into their own ad set with warm audiences and the right exclusions (never
   shown to cold traffic).

## Gate

Every stage has at least two cells before the slate is called done.

## Fictional example

Fictional business "Aspen Home Solar": unaware cell is "Most people's electric bill goes up every
year whether they use more power or not" (no price, Learn More). Most-aware cell is "Still 6 slots
left for a free-roof-assessment this month in your zip" shown only to site visitors from the last
30 days.
